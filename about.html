THE ARTIST

Mariyam Shahaa Zakee

Mariyam is the artist behind Elaris Art Studio. Her practice is centred on abstraction, gesture and the emotional memory of place.

This first edition of the website deliberately keeps the biography concise until Mariyam's final artist statement, exhibition history and professional portrait are supplied.

ARTIST PORTRAIT

ARTIST STATEMENT · DRAFT

Movement before explanation.

I am interested in the moment where a mark stops being descriptive and starts becoming emotional. The work develops through layers, interruption and instinct, allowing texture and rhythm to hold what words cannot.

Each painting is built through a conversation between control and spontaneity. I want the finished work to feel resolved without becoming closed—to leave space for the viewer's own memory and interpretation.

M. Shahaa
