WORLDWIDE SHIPPING

Worldwide shipping

Original Elaris artworks can be arranged for delivery to collectors and galleries around the world. Every work is handled individually, with careful attention to its scale, surface, framing and final destination.

International shipping

International delivery is arranged according to the artwork and destination. Before dispatch, the studio confirms the most appropriate transport method, packing specification, destination details and expected shipping window.

Tracking and shipment information are shared when available. For destinations requiring customs documentation, the necessary artwork and shipment details are prepared with the carrier before the work leaves the studio.

Packaging

Each work is protected according to its dimensions, materials and finish. Depending on the piece, this can include archival surface protection, corner protection, rigid outer packing or a made-to-measure crate for additional security in transit.

Original works are dispatched with studio documentation and, where applicable, a Certificate of Authenticity.

Delivery in the Maldives

For collectors in the Maldives, local delivery or collection can be arranged directly with the studio. The final arrangement depends on the island, artwork dimensions and the safest transport option available for the work.

For larger pieces, the studio can discuss delivery timing and handling requirements before the artwork is released.

Customs & local charges

International destinations may apply import duties, taxes, brokerage fees or local handling charges. Where these apply, they are normally payable by the recipient and are determined by the rules of the destination country.

Contact & inquiries

For questions about shipping, delivery, packing or a particular artwork, contact the studio directly. Include the artwork you are considering and the destination country so we can discuss the most suitable arrangement.
