WORLDWIDE SHIPPING

From the studio to your space.

Original artworks can be arranged for delivery from the Maldives to collectors around the world.

Every shipment is considered individually. The artwork's scale, surface, framing and destination guide how it is prepared and which delivery method is most appropriate.

01

International delivery

International delivery is arranged around the artwork and its destination. Before dispatch, the studio confirms the delivery method, destination details and expected shipping window.

02

Packaging & protection

Works are protected according to their size, materials and finish. Appropriate wrapping, corner protection, rigid packing or a custom crate may be used where additional protection is needed in transit.

03

Maldives delivery

For collectors in the Maldives, local delivery or collection can be discussed directly with the studio. The most suitable arrangement will depend on the location and dimensions of the work.

04

Customs & local charges

International destinations may apply import duties, taxes, brokerage or local handling charges. Where these apply, they are normally the responsibility of the recipient and are determined by the destination country's rules.

BEFORE DISPATCH

A considered journey for every original work.

Before an artwork leaves the studio, the destination, packing approach and delivery details are checked. Tracking or shipment information is shared when available for the selected service.

Each original work is accompanied by its studio documentation, including a Certificate of Authenticity where applicable.
