MALDIVIAN CONTEMPORARY ARTIST

Mariyam Shahaa is a Maldivian contemporary artist whose abstract practice is driven by emotion, movement and intuition. Through layered surfaces, expressive gesture and shifting texture, her work explores the space between feeling and form.

Her paintings develop through an instinctive process, allowing rhythm, contrast and material to shape each composition. Rather than describing a single image or place, the work invites a personal response — something remembered, sensed or felt.

“I begin with movement, intuition and the traces a feeling leaves behind.”

— Mariyam Shahaa
